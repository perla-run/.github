# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

Report it privately through GitHub: open the repository concerned, go to the **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Only the maintainers can read the report.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the version or commit you tested. We acknowledge reports within three working days and keep you informed until the fix is released.

## Scope

All public repositories of the `perla-run` organization. Test recordings, traces and reports produced by Perla on your own machines are yours: never attach real credentials or customer data to a report.
