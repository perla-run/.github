# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in Perla.

- **Bugs and ideas**: open an issue in the repository concerned, with the steps to reproduce, what you expected and what happened.
- **Pull requests**: keep them small and focused, add a test for the behaviour you change, and make sure the existing test suite passes.
- **Security problems**: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is licensed under the license of the repository you contribute to.
